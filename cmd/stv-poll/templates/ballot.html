How it works

This is a ranked choice poll. You have a Single Transferable Vote (STV). This means every vote counts, and order of preference matters.

Your ranked preferences form one vote.

If your first choice is unpopular, it may be "eliminated". This means your vote moves to your 2nd preference.

This process continues until the poll has a clear result.

A quota is calculated from the number of votes and winning places. Surplus votes and votes for eliminated options move to the next available preference.

For a more detailed description of this method, see Wikipedia's Single Transferable Vote article.
