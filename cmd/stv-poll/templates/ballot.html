How to vote

Number the options in the order you prefer them. Put 1 beside your first choice, 2 beside your second, and continue for as many or as few options as you wish.

Your preferences form one vote. You can return and replace it while voting is open.

How votes are counted

A quota is calculated from the number of votes and winning places. An option reaching the quota is selected.

Surplus votes and votes for excluded options can move to each ballot's next available preference. This lets later preferences help choose the result without creating another vote.
