How to vote

Number the options in the order you prefer them. Put 1 beside your first choice, 2 beside your second, and continue for as many or as few options as you wish.

Your preferences form one vote. While polling is open, you can return and replace your saved preferences before the displayed deadline.

One current ballot is stored per participant. Moderators see aggregate results, and records are retained for review and list reuse.

In a small poll, the results or turnout may allow someone to infer how a person voted or whether they took part.
